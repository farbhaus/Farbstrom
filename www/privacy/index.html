Privacy & Cookies

Functional storage only — no analytics, no tracking.

This platform is a private streaming tool. It does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking, and it sets no third-party cookies. The only data stored in your browser is strictly functional — it exists so the app works and remembers your choices on this device. None of it is sent to anyone other than the server that runs this room.

What is stored on your device

  • Session — a short-lived access token and your participant ID for the room you are in, so you stay connected across a page refresh. Cleared when you close the browser tab.
  • Preferences — per-room settings such as your camera/microphone choice, audio options, and push-to-talk, so they persist the next time you join the same room.
  • Convenience — your display name (and, if you tick the box, the room password) so you don't have to re-enter them. You control this and can clear it at any time.

Cookies

No tracking or advertising cookies are used. The items above are kept in your browser's local and session storage, not in cookies, and are used only to operate the service.

Clearing your data

Session storage is cleared automatically when you close the tab. Anything kept between visits can be removed at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of this deployment (the team that shared the room link with you).
